What we print.

Paper packaging is most of what we do. Gift boxes, labels and printed matter come from the same presses.

  • Folding cartons

    Our main line. Cartons for food, coffee, tea, cosmetics and cigarettes, printed on Heidelberg presses, then die-cut and glued on automatic lines. Inline coating protects the surface and saves a pass on the press.

    Duplex, Ivory, Couché, Bristol board

  • Metallized packaging

    Board laminated with silver or gold metallized film, with a smoother and brighter surface than standard metallized paper. Inline coating brings out detail and colour on premium products.

    150 to 400 gsm, silver or gold

  • Labels

    Paper and sticker labels with UV or water-based coating that resists scratches and smudges and is safe for food packaging.

    Couché, sticker paper, gloss or matte BOPP

  • Rigid boxes and paper bags

    Gift boxes, mooncake boxes, tea and wine sets, shopping bags. Board wrapped in printed paper, with custom inserts, magnetic lids and ribbon handles.

    Foil, embossing, spot UV, soft-touch or gloss lamination

  • Magazines, calendars and books

    Magazines, catalogues, calendars and books. You approve a colour proof before the run, then we finish and bind.

    80 to 400 gsm, saddle stitch, perfect or spiral binding

Colour on target.

We measure colour instead of judging it by eye. Prepress and presses are linked through CIP4, sheets are read with X-Rite instruments, and inks are mixed to L*a*b* targets within a set ΔE.

The settings for every job are saved, so a reprint next season matches the first run. Our process is certified to ISO 12647 and audited by GMI for retailers in the United States.

Heidelberg from plate to press.

Minh Phuong Printing was founded in 2006 to raise the standard of printing in Vietnam. Since then we have built a new factory, kept investing in Heidelberg presses and prepress, and adopted the colour and quality rules that international brands expect.

In 2012 we received GMI certification, which lets us supply retailers in the United States.

We also help clients plan their packaging: choosing the board, the structure and the finish so the product looks right on the shelf and runs smoothly in production.

How a job runs.

Every order follows the same five stages, managed in Heidelberg Prinect from the first sample to the last pallet.

  1. 1

    Sample

    We draw the structure in ArtiosCAD and cut a white sample on the Kongsberg table, so you can check size and fit before anything is printed.

  2. 2

    Prepress

    Files are checked, colour-managed and trapped, imposed in Prinect Signa Station and output to plate on a Heidelberg Suprasetter.

  3. 3

    Printing

    Heidelberg Speedmaster presses with inline coating. X-Rite measurement keeps colour on target, and the settings are saved for reprints.

  4. 4

    Finishing

    Lamination, hot foil, embossing and spot UV, then die-cutting, folding and gluing.

  5. 5

    Inspection and delivery

    An automatic inspection line checks graphics, colour and cut position. Then we pack, palletise and deliver.
